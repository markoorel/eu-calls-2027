What do the EU terms mean?
RIA (Research and Innovation Action)
A project that creates new knowledge or tests a new approach; under the standard rule it needs at least three organisations from three different countries.
IA (Innovation Action)
A project closer to practice, such as piloting or demonstrating a new product, service or process, with the same standard consortium rule as an RIA.
CSA (Coordination and Support Action)
Funding for networking, coordination, policy support or dissemination rather than research itself; under the standard rule a single organisation may apply.
Lump sum
The grant is a fixed amount agreed in advance and paid when the planned work is done, so you report on results instead of every cost.
Two-stage
You first send a short proposal; only applicants who pass that stage are invited to submit a full proposal by the second deadline.
Cluster
One of six themed parts of Horizon Europe's collaborative research; Cluster 2, for example, covers culture, creativity and inclusive society.
Destination
A group of related topics within a cluster that aim at the same expected impacts.
ERC panel
One of 28 subject panels that evaluate ERC proposals; applicants choose the panel closest to their research, such as SH1 for economics, finance and management.

    About this data

    Calls are copied each night from the public search service behind the EU Funding & Tenders Portal. Where a value is missing here, the page says “check portal”. “Who applies” follows the general rule for each type of action and can differ for a particular topic. Department matches come from search terms in each call's title and description: they suggest where to look and do not judge whether a call suits you. The page uses no cookies, tracking or analytics.
